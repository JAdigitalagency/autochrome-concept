FAQ

Good to know.

What areas do you cover?

Autochrome serves Plymouth, Plympton, Saltash, Ivybridge and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether you're covered, get in touch and Autochrome will confirm.

How do I get a quote?

Call 07814 646910 or message Autochrome on Facebook. Quotes are free, and photos of your wheels help.

Do you do end of lease repairs?

Yes. Kerb damaged wheels, smart repairs and bodywork brought back to factory finish before you return the car, with packages from £50. Short notice jobs are welcome.

What finishes and colours can I choose?

Gloss, satin, matt and more, plus custom colours such as Metallic Carbon and Shadow Chrome. Want to see samples of each colour? Just ask.

Do you work on all cars?

Yes, all makes and models are welcome.

How long does it take?

It depends on the wheels and the work needed. Same day turnaround is available on some jobs, so ask when you get your quote.
