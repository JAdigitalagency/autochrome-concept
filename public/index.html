FAQ

Good to know.

What areas do you cover?

Autochrome provides a mobile service across Plymouth and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether you're covered, get in touch and Autochrome will confirm.

How do I get a quote?

Call 07814 646910 or message Autochrome on Facebook. Quotes are free.

Can I send photos of my damaged wheels?

Yes — photos are the easiest way to show the condition of your wheels when you ask for a quote.

Do you offer a mobile service?

Yes. Autochrome is a mobile alloy wheel refurbishment service, with the work arranged at an agreed location.

How long does refurbishment take?

It depends on the wheels and the work needed. Get in touch with a few photos and Autochrome will confirm.
